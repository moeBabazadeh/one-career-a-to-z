## What I'll write about

The same things the rest of this site is about:

- **Platforms:** what I learn building software at work, where I can share it.
- **Firmware and hardware:** boards, modems, sensors and the code that runs on them.
- **Side projects:** small tools I write for myself and then put on [GitHub](https://github.com/moeBabazadeh).

Most posts will be practical: what I built, what went wrong, and what I'd do differently next time.
